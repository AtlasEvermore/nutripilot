Response to Guideline 2.1 - Information Needed

1) SCREEN RECORDING: attached (physical iPhone, latest iOS, TestFlight build 401). It starts at app launch and shows the main tabs (Today, food search, Training demos, Apps, Progress), Settings > Delete my account, the fresh onboarding questionnaire after deletion, and Plans with the App Store subscription price. The app has no user-generated content shared with other users (no feed, comments or messaging between users), so no reporting/blocking is needed.

2) PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE: NutriPilot is a nutrition and wellbeing app for adults who want to lose weight sustainably and improve daily quality of life, including women 40+ in menopause. Problem: generic diets ignore each person's routine, so people give up. From a short questionnaire (goal, routine, food preferences) the app builds a personal plan: daily calorie and macro targets, meal logging with a multilingual food database and barcode scanner, 228 recipes, 107 home exercises, hydration and sleep tracking, a menopause space and a coach. Health content is general information and the app states that it does not replace medical advice.

3) HOW TO ACCESS: No login is needed to explore. Open the app, choose a language, answer the questionnaire (about 1 minute) and you land on Today. Tabs: Today (calorie ring, add meal, water), Apps (all spaces), Scanner, Plans, Progress. Free: questionnaire, daily targets, manual food logging, water, barcode scanner. Paid: tap Plans and choose the monthly or annual auto-renewing subscription (Apple In-App Purchase only, no external payment links). Before the first purchase the app asks the user to sign in so the subscription can be linked and restored on other devices: Sign in with Apple (when shown), or enter any e-mail address and type the 6-digit code received by e-mail. Restore purchases is in Settings (also on the Plans screen). Account deletion: Settings > Delete my account (also https://app.atlasevermore.com/delete-account). No demo credentials are needed; any e-mail address works.

4) EXTERNAL SERVICES: Apple In-App Purchase / StoreKit with RevenueCat (subscription status); Sign in with Apple; Resend (sends the sign-in code e-mail); OpenAI API (coach answers and photo meal analysis for subscribers, only after the user gives consent, which can be turned off in Settings); Open Food Facts (barcode product data, ODbL licence, credited in the app); open.er-api.com (exchange rates for approximate local-currency prices; the price charged is always the one in the App Store purchase sheet); Railway (hosting of the app server at app.atlasevermore.com).

5) REGIONAL DIFFERENCES: The app works the same in all regions where it is available. It is localized in 14 languages; only the language and the currency of App Store prices change.

6) REGULATED INDUSTRY / THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL: NutriPilot is a general wellness app, not a medical device; it does not diagnose, treat or prescribe and does not connect to health providers. Recipes, exercises, texts and artwork were created by the developer. Food product data comes from Open Food Facts under the Open Database License, with attribution in the app. No protected third-party material is used.
